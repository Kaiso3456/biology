Section D — Structured Answers

Question 1: Xylem and Phloem

  • Xylem transports water and mineral ions.
  • Xylem vessels are generally hollow at maturity and have lignified walls.
  • Phloem transports organic assimilates such as sucrose.
  • Sieve tube elements are associated with companion cells.
  • Explain the structure and function of each tissue for full marks.

Question 2: Movement of Water

  • Water is absorbed from soil by root hairs.
  • Water enters root hair cells by osmosis.
  • It moves across the cortex through apoplast and symplast pathways.
  • Water enters the xylem and moves towards the leaves.
  • Water evaporates from mesophyll surfaces and diffuses out through stomata.
